Why this wayObjectives are written as behaviors the learner can do on a call, not topics they will see. Saying up front how scoring works removes guesswork. The SCORM box says what the standard is for in two sentences, and the panel it links to shows the real values.The line mirrors what executives said under investor pressure. Starting with it makes the learner's first decision the central skill.New sellers don't need an AI taxonomy. They need a quick way to decide what to ask next. Three zones is small enough to remember on a call.Sellers frame the conversation and bring in a solutions architect for the comparison. The planner models that division of labor and avoids model names that would go stale.Feature tours lose technical buyers. The scenario rewards questions about cost, speed, quality, and security reviews.This is the conversation the case is named for. The strong path defines success first, then proves it in a pilot.Capacity was a real constraint in 2024 and 2025. Treating it as a conversation skill keeps the content useful and avoids internal allocation detail.Questions test decisions, not recall of product facts.The job aid is the part reps will actually reuse.
